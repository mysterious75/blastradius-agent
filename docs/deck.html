02 / 08 · The problem

DeFi is composable.
So is its failure.

Every protocol that whitelists a token makes an implicit assumption: that the token stays backed. When that assumption breaks, the loss does not stay in one protocol. It cascades through every market holding it, every protocol hosting those markets, and every chain carrying a wrapper.

03 / 08 · The incident
$292M

One verifier. Zero contract bugs.

18 April 2026: a cross-chain receive pathway configured with a single required verifier accepted a forged packet and released 116,500 rsETH. Every contract behaved exactly as designed. Downstream: frozen markets, unliquidatable bad debt in a major lending pool, wrapped copies on 20+ chains left questioning their backing.

06 / 08 · Moat

The asset is the graph,
not the tool.

Tool-only products in this market get acqui-hired or sunset. What compounds is a continuously maintained dependency graph: which token is listed as collateral where, how much is supplied, how much is borrowed against it, which chains carry a wrapper, which verifier stack stands behind the bridge.

That graph is what makes the next listing's answer instant instead of a research project.
